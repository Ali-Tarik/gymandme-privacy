GYM & ME — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

GYM & ME does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Your data

  • Everything you enter (workouts, routines, bodyweight, profile, custom exercises, videos and pictures) is stored only on your device.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it makes no network connection.
  • Files leave your device only when you choose: a backup file is saved where you pick, and a shared routine file goes only to the app or person you send it to.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, permanently deletes all of its data. You can back up and restore your data from the app's Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional, asked once): to show the workout in progress and the rest timer on the lock screen. If you refuse, everything else works the same.
  • Granted automatically at install (no prompt, no data leaves your phone): keep the workout running in the background (foreground service, and the sensor-access permission Android requires for this type of workout service), keep the screen and timer awake (wake lock) and vibrate when a rest ends.
  • Some system components also declare the standard "view network status" permission; the app never uses the internet.
  • Videos are recorded with your phone's own camera app or chosen with the system photo picker. Only the clip you select is copied into the app's private storage.

Children

  • The app is not directed at children under 13.
